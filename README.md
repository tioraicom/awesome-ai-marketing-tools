The question this list answers is narrow on purpose: what can actually do a piece of marketing
work? Not what could a marketer conceivably open. A general chat assistant will write you a
headline, which is why it appears on every marketing tools list published and why those lists
end up identical to each other.

Categories follow the work rather than the software market, so attribution and conversion
optimisation get as much room as content generation. Search tools are covered separately in
the TiorAI portfolio and are not repeated here, which keeps this list about campaigns,
channels, and demand.

Maintained by [TiorAI](https://tiorai.com/), which catalogues AI tools for a living.

## Content creation and copywriting

Producing campaign copy and long-form content with brand context attached.

- **[Anyword](https://anyword.com/)** — Scores copy against predicted performance for a specific audience before you publish it. `Paid` `Web` `Browser Extension`
- **[Copy.ai](https://www.copy.ai/)** — Campaign copy across formats, with workflows that chain steps rather than one prompt box. `Freemium` `Web` `API` · [vs Jasper](https://tiorai.com/compare/copy-ai-vs-jasper-ai/)
- **[Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai/)** — Content platform built around brand voice and campaign templates, aimed at marketing teams. `Paid` `Web` `API` `Browser Extension` · [alternatives](https://tiorai.com/alternatives/jasper-ai-alternatives/)
- **[Rytr](https://rytr.me/)** — Short-form copy generator with a free tier, useful for ads and product descriptions. `Freemium` `Web` `Browser Extension`
- **[Typeface](https://www.typeface.ai/)** — Enterprise content generation that enforces brand assets and tone across every output. `Paid` `Web` `API`
- **[Wordtune](https://www.wordtune.com/)** — Rewrites a line several ways, which is faster than regenerating a whole draft for tone. `Freemium` `Web` `Browser Extension`
- **[Writer](https://writer.com/)** — Enforces a house style guide across everything a marketing team publishes, with its own models. `Paid` `Web` `API` `Browser Extension`
- **[Writesonic](https://writesonic.com/)** — Content generation with search data and brand voice attached, aimed at publishing volume. `Freemium` `Web` `API` · [vs Jasper](https://tiorai.com/compare/writesonic-vs-jasper-ai/)

## Social media

- **[Metricool](https://metricool.com/)** — Scheduling plus analytics across channels, including competitor benchmarks on the same dashboard. `Freemium` `Web` `iOS` `Android`
- **[Ocoya](https://www.ocoya.com/)** — Generates the copy and the creative together, then schedules both across channels. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Publer](https://publer.com/)** — Bulk workflows and caption generation across many accounts, priced for small teams. `Freemium` `Web` `iOS` `Android`

- **[Foreplay](https://www.foreplay.co/)** — Swipe file for competitor ad creative, organised and searchable across brands. `Paid` `Web` `Browser Extension`
- **[Klue](https://klue.com/)** — Competitive intelligence platform that delivers battlecards into the CRM where deals happen. `Paid` `Web`
- **[Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/)** — Traffic and audience estimates for any site, with channel breakdowns for competitor analysis. `Freemium` `Web` `API` `Browser Extension`

## How tools are selected

Three tests, and a tool has to pass all three.

**Is marketing a primary use case?** Not a possible one. The vendor builds for marketers, the
pricing is aimed at marketing teams, and the product would make no sense to anyone else.

**Does it do a specific job?** A channel, a funnel stage, a campaign artefact, a measurement.
"Helps with marketing" is not a job.

**Could a marketer put it into a real workflow this quarter?** Products that require an
implementation project before they do anything are excluded unless the capability is genuinely
unavailable elsewhere.

What is deliberately elsewhere: search work of every kind lives in `awesome-ai-seo-tools`, and
everyday knowledge work lives in `awesome-ai-productivity-tools`. A tool that genuinely serves
two of those audiences appears in both, described for that audience rather than copied across.

What is excluded outright: general chat assistants, generic writing tools with no campaign or
brand context, and agency services sold as software.

Pricing skews `Paid` in this list more than anywhere else in the portfolio. That is not a
selection bias; it is what this category is like, and pretending otherwise by padding with
free tools nobody uses professionally would be less useful.

Entries are ordered alphabetically inside each category. Position carries no meaning, and
there is no paid placement, sponsorship, or affiliate link anywhere in this repository.

## Disclaimer

Everything listed is built and operated by a third party, not by TiorAI. Pricing, plan limits,
and availability change without notice, and this category consolidates faster than most:
products are acquired and folded into suites regularly. Check current terms before committing
a team or a budget.

Several of these tools process customer data, contact lists, or advertising accounts. Check
where that data goes, and what the vendor may do with it, before connecting anything.
Listing here is editorial selection, not endorsement.
